## 🌌 What Is GravityGarden?

Think of Descenders as a mountain bike. Now imagine you had the keys to the mountain itself — the dirt, the wind, the gravity, the very laws that make a descent feel like falling through a dream. **GravityGarden** is that keyring.

This is not a mod menu. It's a **physics sandbox studio** built on the backbone of MelonLoader, designed for the Offline/Bike Park experience. It lets you sculpt the invisible forces that govern every jump, every landing, and every glorious wipeout. Whether you're a casual trail-drifter or a professional line-optimizer, GravityGarden transforms your game into an experimental laboratory where **momentum, friction, and airtime become adjustable ingredients in your personal recipe of flow**.

Every great descent is a symphony of unseen forces. GravityGarden pulls back the curtain on that orchestra — allowing you to **retune the violins of torque**, **soften the percussion of landing impacts**, and **rewrite the sheet music of terrain interaction**.

## ⚙️ Feature Arsenal

### 🚴 Physical Tuning Suite
- **Gravity Coefficients** — Adjust global pull from *feather-drifting* to *moon-hop* to *planetary-crush*.
- **Torque & Traction Curves** — Fine-tune grip on dirt, asphalt, and pump track surfaces.
- **Suspension Dynamics** — Modify rebound, compression, and travel for clicky or floaty suspensions.
- **Impact Damping** — Control how energy is absorbed on drops (or explode on impact for comedic effect).
- **Wind Vector Fields** — Direct ambient airflow that pushes riders mid-air, adding an entirely new layer of strategy.

### 🎭 Custom Game Modes
- **Glacier Drift** — Ice physics apply to all surfaces; momentum is king, brakes are suggestions.
- **Quantum Locust** — Bike mass randomly redistributes every 30 seconds. Survive the chaos.
- **The Long Fall** — Doubles vertical scale while keeping horizontal speed — a test of nerve.
- **Time-Warp Trials** — Slow-motion or hyper-speed global timers for meticulous line practice.

### 👻 Ghost Replay Engine
- **Precision Recording** — Capture every pedal stroke and subtle lean angle.
- **Layered Comparisons** — Race against your past five attempts simultaneously.
- **Separated Axis Playback** — Watch your ghost on X-axis but follow a friend's Y-axis for learning.
- **Physics Ghost Exaggeration** — Visualize your bumpy line vs. your current smooth line.

### 🎨 World Illumination & Vibe
- **Skybox Alchemy** — Swap day/night, storm systems, and aurora colors.
- **Terrain Tint** — Alter trail rims, signage, and distant mountains to enhance visibility.
- **Rain Ripple Simulator** — Visual-only wetness overlay (physics optional via separate slider).

### 🧩 Modular UI Framework
- **Responsive Radial Menu** — Touch-friendly design for Steam Deck, mouse for desktop.
- **Drag-and-Drop Preset System** — Save your favorite chaos recipes as `.ggarden` files.
- **Live Multi-Axis Graph** — Visualize your current speed/torque/friction coefficients in real-time.

## 🛠️ Installation & Integration

*No terminal gymnastics required.* Follow the breadcrumb trail:

1. **Obtain the Core Library** — Ensure MelonLoader is present (v0.6.1+).
2. **Place the ModKit** — Unzip `GravityGarden` into your `Mods` folder.
3. **Launch & Load** — Start Descenders in Offline/Bike Park mode; press `F8` to summon the main garden panel.

> **Compatibility** — Built for Steam builds (2026 edition). Works exclusively in single-player/sandbox environments.
